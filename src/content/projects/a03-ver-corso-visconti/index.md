  # I. Site investigation — the history that motivates the proposal
  - text: "Scattered within the fortress walls, Lecco's historical timestamps present the old ways of life evolved through the ages. As an important military base, an artificial island — the Isola Viscontea — and a stone bridge — the Ponte Azzone Visconti — were built as major defensive control points on the River Adda. Today the two monuments are disregarded: the former a private island inaccessible to the public, the latter a purely functional passage across the river. Their historical significance seems to have been forgotten over contemporary urban developments."
    smallText: true

  - video: "https://youtu.be/h7NIYDp5nbs"
    autoplay: true

  # II. Our proposal
  - text: "The proposal engages the public so that these places of special value are recognized and connected as a historical trail: seating abutting the river, lush greenery of a multitude of flora and fauna, a public plaza with commercial services, steps down to the pristine water, a bridge to the island's museum, a car-free Ponte Visconti, and a completed bike lane connecting Maggianico, Malgrate, and Pescate, with new levels of viewpoints towards the city and nature."
    smallText: true
